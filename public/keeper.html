How we verify

Received rings record verified two-chat claims or explicitly labelled Wander idle transfers. They do not measure retained viewers or conference attendance. Presence and audience are not proof of new or retained viewers.

Records cover retained completed stops only; earlier holds and raider participation may be unknown. Chat location is not proof of raid membership. Only approved engravings are public; pending text and private participant IDs are not shown here.

Saved downloads cannot be recalled after erasure. Each download checks the current public record again.

The only creator test channels are raidrelichq, olsols2 and olsols3. Owner-operated test raids are not community adoption. Operator admission and broadcaster consent remain required.
