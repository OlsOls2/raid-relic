# Judge in 60 seconds

[Demo](https://raid-relic-760724762396.us-central1.run.app/demo/) · [Recorded Atlas](https://raid-relic-760724762396.us-central1.run.app/atlas/)

No credentials, Twitch login or installation needed. Press **Autoplay 35-second tour**, or use Send Off → an early source offer → receipt → distinct host accept. Eligible offers may be retained before arrival; watch the meter and resend after arrival if yours was not counted. Pause to take over with the keyboard, then replay or reset. Binding—not merely choosing a source—enables the simulated card download. All demo state is local fiction, with no API/socket/provider/storage work and a permanent banner/watermark.

Open the Atlas and replay Stop 8. Try a recorded ring-card download, inspect the Keeper ledger and compare completed stops. Cosmetic edition dates differ from event-days badges; neither proves attendance. A legacy Chain of 3 badge does not prove an unbroken streak: older failed-window history is unknown. Only new covered completions count toward tracked streak badges.

For streamers, the benefit is continuity: two communities leave approved marks on one shared relic and pass its growing history onward. [Owner-configured installation and permissions](JUDGE_SCOPES.md). Public readers do not opt into scores. Marks are five per viewer per stream, with a 30-second cooldown. Signed moderator/broadcaster veto and self-forget remove retained content without refunding quota. A downloaded PNG cannot be recalled.
