## Controls are different

Public content browsing is read-only; **production is not globally read-only**. Keeper control requires Twitch sign-in as one of the owner-admitted creator channels raidrelichq, olsols2 or olsols3. Arbitrary judges do not gain controls. Viewing public pages grants no consent, live setup or raid authority and is not open public signup. Existing authorised owner Twitch controls retain their identity, session, role, Origin/CSRF, consent and provider-readiness checks. Judges are not instructed to trigger Twitch actions.

API acceptance is not arrival. A verified eligible receipt and both eligible chats must complete the180-second claim window before a transfer. Chat location does not prove raid membership. Only approved marks are public; pending text and private viewer identifiers remain private. Retained approved marks survive offline subject to forget/block/veto/opt-out privacy removals. Forgetting does not refund quota; downloaded files cannot be recalled. These three creator test channels are not community-adoption evidence.
